## Blocker

### QA-042: Optional pickers show a blank choice but submit the first option
- **Area:** dashboard (Billing, Trust & accounting, Portal, Intake and every form that uses `SearchableOptionSelect`)
- **URL:** `/dashboard/platform/billing`, `/dashboard/platform/accounting`, `/dashboard/platform/portal`
- **Steps:**
  1. In Billing → New rate card, leave **Matter** on "Firm-wide". Create the card.
  2. In Record payment, leave **Payer** on "Invoice client". Record a payment against QA-INV-0001, which is QA Prospect One's invoice.
  3. In Accounting → Open client ledger, look at **Matter** ("Client-level ledger") and **Client** ("Matter client").
- **Expected:** A blank choice submits blank: a firm-wide card, the invoice's own client, a client-level ledger.
- **Actual:**
  - The rate card was created for matter **QA-2026-001**. Both QA cards show "Matter QA-2026-001".
  - The payment's payer is **QA Client Two** (see `/dashboard/payments`), a different client from the invoice's.
  - On load, I compared what each picker shows with what it submits (`FormData`):

    | Picker | Shows | Submits |
    |---|---|---|
    | Rate card **Matter** | "Firm-wide" | QA-2026-001 |
    | Rate rule **Practice area** | "Any practice area" | QA Estate Planning |
    | Trust account **Office** | "No office" | QA Downtown Office |
    | Ledger **Matter** | "Client-level ledger" | QA-2026-001 |
    | Ledger **Client** | "Matter client" | QA Client Two |
    | Portal invite **Contact** | "Select…" | QA Adverse Sibling |
  - "Firm-wide" and the other blank choices can't be picked from the list at all.
  - This silently writes wrong financial and trust data, such as a trust ledger under the wrong client.
- **Cause:**
  - `components/ui/form-select.tsx:33` drops the `value=""` option from the Radix items.
  - Radix's hidden native `<select>` then has no empty option, so the browser submits its first option.
  - `initial` on line 19 is `''`, so the trigger shows the placeholder while the form posts option 1.
- **Likely files:** `components/ui/form-select.tsx`, `features/platform/components/SearchableOptionSelect.tsx:83-95`

### QA-047: A validation error on Public site wipes the owner's unsaved edits and switches

- **URL:** `/dashboard/platform/public-site`

- **Actual:**
